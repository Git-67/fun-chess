    Welcome to the chaos.

    Capture the enemy king with a chess piece to win. There is no check restriction. Kings begin with one shield; spells, towers and hazards can remove that shield or stun them, but cannot kill them outright.

    • A move earns 1 AP, capped at 5. Every card costs its AP and uses one action. Overcharge is the only card that earns AP.
    • Each turn, choose one safe action or a 50/50 coin toss. Heads grants two actions; tails forfeits your turn. The coin lands after two seconds. Moving or selecting a card chooses safe automatically.
    • Four cards rotate through a 24-card deck. Both armies start with the same hand, queue and resources. Bot difficulty changes its decisions, not its resources.
    • At most two towers per army. A new tower needs its deployment turn to build. Then it fires after your completed turns. Cannons have range 3, lasers range 5; both hit one enemy and have three shots before crumbling. Allies block shots.
    • Pac-Man follows a fixed six-step route. Yellow pellets connect the route; STOP shows its final square. It takes one step per completed turn after a build-up turn. Shields and protected kings can stop it early.
    • Dino advances exactly one square per completed turn, after a build-up turn, and can enter from either edge, exiting at the opposite edge. Hazards attack either army. Capture one by moving a chess piece onto it.
    • There can be at most two roaming hazards. Random dangers start after round 2. Meteors, volcanoes and random weather announce their target so both armies get a turn to react.
    • Shields absorb one hit and prevent Turncoat. A shielded chess target and its attacker stay put after an attack; that move still earns 1 AP. Earthquakes deal 3 damage to towers and freeze other units.
    • Every third round, optional quick-time events offer +1 AP or one shield. They start automatically; you have six seconds. Miss or skip with no penalty. Solo timers pause when the app is hidden; online timers continue to keep both players synchronized. The bot gets the same reward opportunity with a fixed 65% success rate on every difficulty.
    • Choose Easy, Normal or Hard in Game options. Normal considers immediate threats; Hard also searches replies. Adjust sound volume or turn quick-time events off there.
    • Health: pawn 2, knight/bishop 3, rook 4, queen 5, king 6; cannon/laser 3 and wall 4. Attacks deal 2 damage, or 3 for rooks, queens and lasers. A surviving defender and attacker stay put. Shields absorb a hit; environmental damage cannot kill a king.
    • Castling: select an unmoved king and move two squares toward an unmoved rook. The path must be clear, unfrozen, and safe from enemy chess attacks. En passant: after an enemy pawn’s double-step, capture diagonally into its passed square on your immediately following turn. With HP, a surviving pawn stays in place. Promotion preserves damage already taken.
    • Undo restores the earlier board and random seed, including the coin result.

    Sound can be enabled with the speaker button. Volume defaults to 100%, with amplified effects. Your progress and options save on this device where browser storage is available.
